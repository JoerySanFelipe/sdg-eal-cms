Upstream Responsibility & Watershed Protection

Urdaneta City University (UCU) recognizes that marine health begins inland. In pursuit of SDG 14, we emphasize the prevention of land-based pollution from reaching provincial river systems and eventually the Lingayen Gulf. Our comprehensive Zero-Waste-to-Landfill policy and campus-wide ban on single-use plastics serve as critical barriers against the flow of microplastics into the aquatic food chain.

Furthermore, our centralized wastewater treatment facility ensures that 100% of campus effluent is rigorously neutralized before discharge. By maintaining high-quality output standards, we protect localized freshwater biodiversity and contribute to the broader goal of maintaining healthy marine ecosystems upstream.

Aquatic Research and Community Advocacy

Our academic departments actively engage in research focused on freshwater ecology and the impact of agricultural runoff on local water bodies. Through our External Office, we conduct community seminars on responsible chemical use in farming, aimed at reducing the nitrogen and phosphorus loading that leads to downstream eutrophication and marine "dead zones."
