Circular Campus Operations

Urdaneta City University (UCU) has institutionalized a comprehensive circular economy framework to ensure that our consumption patterns are both responsible and sustainable. Our "Zero-Waste-to-Landfill" policy mandates rigorous segregation at the source, supported by our Material Recovery Facility (MRF) which processes all solid waste generated on campus.

By prioritizing the upcycling of plastics and the on-campus composting of organic cafeteria waste, we have achieved an 85% waste diversion rate. This ensures that our operational footprint remains minimal, directly contributing to the preservation of our regional ecosystem.

Ethical Procurement & Digitalization

In addition to waste management, UCU has overhauled its supply chain through an ethical procurement policy that favors eco-certified vendors and minimizes single-use packaging. Our transition to 90% paperless administrative operations has further reduced institutional demand for raw timber products, aligning our internal logistics with global sustainability benchmarks.
