Institutionalizing Gender Equity

Urdaneta City University enforces a strict, merit-based framework that actively dismantles gender-based disparities across student admissions, faculty hiring, and executive appointments. Operating under the rigorous oversight of our Gender and Development (GAD) office, we ensure a safe, inclusive, and empowering campus environment for all identities.

The university maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy against gender-based discrimination, harassment, and violence. Our institutional grievance mechanisms are engineered to provide immediate, confidential, and highly effective legal and psychological support networks.

Advancing Women in STEM and Leadership

Historically male-dominated fields—such as engineering, information technology, and institutional governance—have been deliberately restructured at UCU to maximize female participation. Through targeted scholarship pipelines, peer-mentorship protocols, and leadership incubation programs, the university has systematically eliminated the gender gap in technical disciplines.

Today, UCU boasts a female-majority representation in academic administration and continuously graduates top-tier female professionals who dictate innovation within the regional workforce.
