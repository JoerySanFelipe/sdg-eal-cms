Holistic Campus Health Ecosystem

Urdaneta City University asserts that academic excellence is fundamentally rooted in the physical and mental well-being of its constituents. To advance SDG 3, we have fortified our institutional health infrastructure, ensuring that all students, faculty, and staff have immediate, free access to primary medical care, dental services, and mental health counseling through the enhanced University Clinic.

Beyond treating acute ailments, our health policy is aggressively preventative. The university mandates comprehensive annual health assessments, supports active intramural athletic programs, and enforces strict campus-wide sanitation and bio-security protocols to prevent the outbreak of communicable diseases.

Community Medical & Extension Missions

The expertise housed within our College of Nursing and College of Pharmacy extends far beyond the classroom. UCU operates a robust extension program that delivers critical medical interventions to Geographically Isolated and Disadvantaged Areas (GIDA) within the region.

Through our student-led medical missions, we provide maternal health seminars, pediatric immunizations, basic pharmaceutical dispensing, and hypertension screenings. These initiatives not only bridge the healthcare gap for vulnerable populations but also forge highly competent, socially conscious medical professionals.
