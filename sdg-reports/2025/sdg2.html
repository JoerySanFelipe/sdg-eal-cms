Food Security and Campus Nutrition

Urdaneta City University recognizes that cognitive development and academic success are fundamentally tied to nutritional security. In pursuit of SDG 2, our institution has mobilized aggressive campus-wide feeding programs and subsidized cafeteria initiatives to guarantee that no student attends lectures in a state of food insecurity.

By leveraging our agricultural departments, we have transformed unused campus acreage into high-yield urban farming incubators. These hubs not only provide fresh, organic produce directly to the university cafeterias but also serve as living laboratories for sustainable agronomy.
