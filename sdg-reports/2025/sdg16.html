Institutional Transparency & Accountability

Urdaneta City University (UCU) upholds the highest standards of institutional integrity as a foundation for academic excellence. To advance SDG 16, we have formalized a Strong Institution Framework that ensures absolute transparency in administrative operations, financial management, and policy development. Our "Open-Door" governance model invites scrutiny and encourages active participation from all sectors of the university community.

The university maintains a zero-tolerance policy regarding unethical conduct, supported by robust internal auditing units and an independent ethics committee. This ensures that UCU remains an accountable, safe, and just environment where the rights of every student and staff member are fiercely protected.

Inclusive Representation & Civic Engagement

Inclusive decision-making is central to our campus culture. We empower 24 recognized student organizations and the Central Student Council to act as legitimate representatives in university board meetings, ensuring that student voices directly shape institutional directives. Beyond the campus, our External Office facilitates legal clinics and civic awareness seminars, promoting the rule of law and peaceful conflict resolution within our regional partner communities.
