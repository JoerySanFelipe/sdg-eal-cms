Institutional Economic Resilience

Urdaneta City University (UCU) serves as a vital economic engine for the region, prioritizing decent work and sustainable growth both within our campus and throughout our partner communities. Through our Earn-While-You-Learn (EWYL) initiative, we provide marginalized students with living wages and professional experience, effectively removing financial barriers to graduation while contributing to the local labor market.

Our commitment extends to absolute compliance with national labor standards. We ensure that 100% of our faculty and staff operate under fair-wage contracts with full social security benefits, fostering a culture of professional dignity and long-term institutional stability.

Community Livelihood Support

The External Office and Linkages actively bridges the gap between academic theory and economic practice. Our technical support for the DSWD’s Kalahi-CIDSS Cash-for-Work Program is a flagship example of this synergy, where university resources help validate and manage sustainable livelihood projects in vulnerable sectors.
