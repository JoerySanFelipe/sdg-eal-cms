Decarbonizing the Academic Environment

Urdaneta City University (UCU) recognizes the existential threat posed by climate change and has committed to a radical decarbonization strategy. By leveraging the Smart Eco-Campus solar grid, we have successfully reduced our institutional carbon emissions by 30% as of early 2025. This energy transition represents our primary offensive against climate change, moving us closer to our 2030 Net-Zero operational target.

Our climate action extends to institutional policy; we have mandated a "Low Carbon" operational framework that prioritizes energy-efficient architecture, digital administrative processes, and the preservation of our campus green canopy to act as a localized carbon sink.

Climate Resilience and Advocacy

Resilience is the second pillar of our strategy. UCU has developed a comprehensive climate adaptation plan, ensuring that our campus infrastructure can withstand extreme weather events common to the region. This includes the integration of advanced storm-water management systems and the establishment of an institutional disaster response command center.

Through our External Office and Linkages, we actively propagate climate awareness to our partner communities, conducting research and seminars on sustainable agriculture and disaster risk reduction, ensuring that our regional impact is as resilient as our campus.
