Social Inclusion and Equitable Access

Urdaneta City University (UCU) actively works to dismantle the systemic barriers that perpetuate social and economic inequality. To advance SDG 10, our institution has implemented a 100% inclusive admission policy, ensuring that students from ethnolinguistic minorities, diverse religious backgrounds, and low-income deciles have an equal seat in our classrooms.

Our External Office and Linkages division extends this commitment to the community by supporting national social protection programs. By providing technical expertise to initiatives like the Kalahi-CIDSS Cash-for-Work program, we help ensure that government aid reaches the most vulnerable sectors with transparency and efficiency.

International Linkages as Equalizers

We believe that global mobility should not be a privilege of the elite. UCU leverages its growing network of international institutional partners to provide exchange opportunities and collaborative research platforms for students who would otherwise lack access to global academic discourse. These linkages serve as powerful equalizers, equipping our graduates with the global competencies required to compete on the international stage.
