Urban Resilience & Green Spaces

Urdaneta City University (UCU) is a vital green lung within the urban landscape of Urdaneta City. Our commitment to SDG 11 is realized through the maintenance of 8.5 hectares of sustainable campus grounds, where architectural development is strictly balanced with ecological preservation. By prioritizing "Forest Campus" initiatives, we provide our community with essential open spaces that foster both mental well-being and biodiversity.

Our campus infrastructure is engineered to be resilient and inclusive. We provide 100% public access to our cultural and academic zones, ensuring that the university serves as a shared community resource rather than an isolated enclave. This integration strengthens the social fabric of our local municipality and promotes a safer, more sustainable urban future.

Sustainable Mobility & Cultural Heritage

In alignment with sustainable mobility targets, UCU has implemented a pedestrian-first policy, reducing vehicular congestion and emissions within the core academic zones. By expanding our network of covered walkways and bicycle-friendly pathways, we promote low-carbon transportation for students and the public alike.
