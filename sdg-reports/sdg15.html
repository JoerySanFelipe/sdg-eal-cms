The Forest Campus Initiative

Urdaneta City University (UCU) remains steadfast in its commitment to campus greenery, ensuring that the physical development of the institution harmonizes with the natural environment. Through our flagship "Forest Campus" initiative, we have aggressively expanded our total area covered in native vegetation, transforming our academic grounds into a thriving terrestrial ecosystem within an urban setting.

The university rigorously monitors its green spaces, recognizing them as vital components of a sustainable campus that supports local biodiversity. This proactive approach to land use management ensures that modern infrastructure development does not come at the expense of environmental integrity, positioning UCU as a leader in regional environmental stewardship.

Biodiversity Preservation and Restoration

Our commitment to SDG 15 extends beyond simple aesthetics. UCU actively maintains extensive open spaces to preserve local ecosystems and provide habitats for indigenous species. By integrating smart architecture and energy-efficient designs, we minimize our spatial footprint, allowing more land to be dedicated to ecological restoration and community-accessible green zones.
